How it compares to other wine apps

There are good wine apps. Most of them answer a different question: which wine should I buy? Vinsieme answers that one too — it researches ratings and market prices, keeps a wishlist and tells you when a price is currently a good one. The difference is that it does not stop there: it stays with the bottle, from the purchase through its slot in the rack and its drinking window to a tasting with guests. Elsewhere that chain only exists spread across several programmes.

Frequently asked

Does it cost anything?

No. Vinsieme is a private, non-commercial project — no ads, no subscription, no selling of data.

Do I need an account?

Not to try it. You only need one once your cellar should sync across devices or be shared with someone.

Does it work in a cellar with no signal?

Yes. Your stock lives on the device: looking things up, taking bottles out and moving them works offline. Only the AI scan and market research need a connection.

How accurate is the label recognition?

Good for common wines, not infallible. Every recognised field can be corrected before saving. Maturity and aroma figures are machine-generated estimates, not advice.

Which devices does Vinsieme run on?

In the browser on any device, and as an Android app. There is no iOS build yet — on iPhone you can add the web app to your home screen.
